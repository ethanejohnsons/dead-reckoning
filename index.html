Flying

Full throttle, hold the centreline with Q/E, ease back at 70 knots. Climb at about 100. The aircraft holds whatever attitude you leave it in, so fly with small taps.

To land: 85 knots with full flaps on approach, power back, and raise the nose just above the runway. Touch down under 500 ft/min.

Navigating

There is no moving map. The briefing gives a heading to steer and a time to fly; the chart pencils in where compass and clock say you should be. Wind you didn't allow for is the difference.

The yellow needle on the compass points at the tuned radio beacon (every airfield has one). Flying over any airfield gives you a position fix.
