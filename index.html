To orbit by hand: lift off, tip the nose east a little at a time (W) so it is on the horizon by 60 miles up, and keep burning until the low point of the orbit is above 90 miles. To the Moon: in orbit, press L and watch; or do it yourself: press 2 and burn until the high point reaches the Moon's path, timed so the Moon is there when you are.
